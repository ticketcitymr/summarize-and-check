The Bank Secrecy Act requires financial institutions to keep records and file reports that help detect and prevent money laundering. Banks must maintain an anti-money laundering program that includes internal controls, independent testing, a designated compliance officer, and ongoing employee training. Suspicious activity reports must be filed with FinCEN when a bank detects transactions that appear to involve illicit funds or have no lawful purpose. Many institutions now use automated transaction monitoring systems to flag unusual patterns for review. These systems generate alerts, and analysts investigate each alert to decide whether a report is warranted. The volume of alerts can be overwhelming, and most turn out to be false positives. Because of this, banks are investing in automation and machine learning to prioritize alerts and reduce manual workload.
